name: cinematic-intro
description: Build an opening sequence that becomes the first usable product state, not a video that hard-cuts to a homepage.
---

Storyboard frame by frame in design/storyboard.md. The last intro frame and the first product frame must share one real thing: a camera position, a geometry, a material, a mask, or a timeline scrub value. Keep the intro mounted until its closing animation finishes (an introFx flag), don't unmount mid-fade. Always ship a skip-intro control.
